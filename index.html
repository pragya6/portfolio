HomeoQuiz

Python · Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite · ChromaDB · sentence-transformers · HF Spaces

Strict-grounding question-answering over a fixed corpus where every answer cites a source passage, with an automated eval harness (Wilson 95% CIs). A guardrail refuses when the source doesn't cover a topic. Hybrid local-embed + remote-generate — a deliberate cost/latency/rate-limit trade-off.
